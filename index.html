What Is This Shorts

A small private tool that publishes videos to the YouTube channel Pip: What Is This?, a channel of short videos about odd objects, weird names and hidden histories, hosted by the cartoon character Pip.

What the tool does

  • Uploads finished videos, made by the channel owner, to the owner's own YouTube channel, and schedules when they go public.
  • Adds each video's title, description, tags, captions and thumbnail.
  • Reads the channel's own view statistics so the owner can choose better topics and posting times.

It runs only on the channel owner's computer and is used only by the channel owner. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account.
